We keep this simple: we collect as little as we can, we never sell it, and you can ask us what we hold about you at any time.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following kinds of personal information when you interact with our website or services:

  • Information you provide. When you contact us or start a project, we ask for your name, email address, and whatever you choose to tell us about your work. We keep it to run the engagement — nothing more.
  • Usage data. Like most websites we receive technical basics — IP address, browser type, pages visited — which we use in aggregate to understand what content is useful.
  • Cookies. We use a small number of first-party cookies to remember preferences and measure, anonymously, how the site is used. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.

2. How we use your information

Your information is used for exactly three things:

  • To do the work. Replying to your enquiry, scoping your project, and delivering the engagement you asked for.
  • To communicate. If you have asked to hear from us, we may send occasional updates. Every message includes a working unsubscribe link, and opting out is instant.
  • To improve the site. Aggregated, anonymized usage patterns tell us which pages earn their place and which need rework.

3. What we never do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not enrich it with data brokers. If a law-enforcement request compels disclosure, we will tell you unless we are legally prevented from doing so.

4. Retention and security

Project records are kept for as long as we work together and for a reasonable period afterwards for legal and accounting purposes. Everything is stored with reputable providers, encrypted in transit and at rest, and accessible only to the people who need it for the work.

5. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We answer every request, usually within a few days and always within the timelines the law requires.
